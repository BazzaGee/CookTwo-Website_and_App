# Low-Sodium / Cardiac-Friendly Diet — Food Framework

## Plate structure

- **½ plate vegetables and fruit** — fresh or frozen plain, no salt added.
- **¼ plate protein** — fish, poultry, legumes, tofu, eggs; limit red meat and avoid processed meat.
- **¼ plate whole grains or starchy vegetables** — brown rice, quinoa, oats, barley, whole-grain bread (low-sodium), potatoes, sweet potatoes.
- **Fat** — olive oil, avocado, unsalted nuts and seeds.
- **Flavour** — herbs, spices (no salt blends), citrus, vinegar, garlic, onion, chilli, ginger.

## Daily nutrient targets

| Nutrient | Target |
|---|---|
| Sodium | ≤1,500 mg/day ideal; ≤2,300 mg absolute ceiling |
| Saturated fat | ≤5–6% of calories |
| Trans fat | ~0 g |
| Fibre | 25–30 g |
| Potassium | 3,500–5,000 mg from food (unless contraindicated) |
| Added sugar | <10% of calories; ideally <5% |
| Fish | 2 servings/week |
| Alcohol | limit or avoid |

**Per-meal planning used by the app:** ~500 mg sodium per main meal supports a 1,500 mg/day pattern across three meals plus small snacks.

## Choose freely

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Vegetables | Fresh or frozen plain vegetables, leafy greens, tomatoes, peppers, squash, broccoli, carrots, beetroot |
| Fruit | All fresh fruit; frozen fruit without syrup |
| Grains | Brown rice, quinoa, oats, barley, bulgur, whole-wheat pasta, unsalted whole-grain bread |
| Legumes | Lentils, chickpeas, black beans, kidney beans — no-salt-added or rinsed |
| Protein | Fresh fish, poultry, lean meat (limit), eggs, tofu, tempeh |
| Dairy | Plain low-fat milk/yogurt; small amounts of lower-sodium cheese |
| Fats | Olive oil, canola oil, avocado, unsalted nuts, unsalted seeds |
| Seasoning | Herbs, spices, garlic, onion, lemon/lime, vinegar, black pepper, chilli |

## Limit or avoid

| Category | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Salt and salty condiments | Table salt, sea salt, soy sauce, tamari, fish sauce, bouillon, stock cubes, gravy, MSG | Sodium |
| Cured and processed meat | Bacon, ham, salami, sausage, deli meat, hot dogs, chorizo | Sodium + saturated fat + nitrates |
| Pickled and brined foods | Pickles, olives, capers, sauerkraut, kimchi | Sodium |
| Canned and instant foods | Canned soup, instant noodles, frozen ready meals, salted canned vegetables | Sodium |
| Salty snacks | Crisps, pretzels, salted crackers, salted popcorn, salted nuts | Sodium |
| High-sodium cheese | Parmesan, feta, halloumi, blue cheese, processed cheese | Sodium + saturated fat |
| Fatty and processed meat | Fatty cuts, burgers, fried chicken | Saturated fat |
| Commercial baked goods | Pastries, croissants, biscuits, cakes | Saturated/trans fat + sodium |
| Sugary drinks | Soft drinks, sweetened juices, energy drinks | Added sugar |
| Alcohol | Beer, wine, spirits | Blood pressure, triglycerides |

## Cooking rules

1. **No salt added** in cooking or at the table.
2. Use **low-sodium or no-salt-added** canned goods; rinse beans and vegetables.
3. Choose **fresh or frozen** over canned whenever possible.
4. Make stock from bones/vegetables without salt, or use low-sodium stock.
5. Season with **herbs, spices, citrus, vinegar, garlic, onion, ginger and chilli**.
6. Read labels: aim for **≤140 mg sodium per serving** for routine foods; ≤5% daily value per serving.
7. Avoid **partially hydrogenated oils** and check for "trans fat" on labels.
8. Grill, bake, steam, poach or sauté with olive oil instead of deep-frying.

## Meal patterns

- **Breakfast:** oats with fruit and unsalted nuts; eggs with spinach and tomato; plain yogurt with berries; low-sodium wholegrain toast with avocado.
- **Lunch:** salad with chickpeas, olive oil and lemon; leftover roast chicken with quinoa and vegetables; lentil soup made without salt.
- **Dinner:** baked salmon with potatoes and broccoli; chicken stir-fry with garlic, ginger and low-sodium tamari (small amount); bean chilli with brown rice.
- **Snacks:** fruit, unsalted nuts, plain yogurt, vegetable sticks with hummus (low-sodium).

## Couple compatibility

- The low-sodium partner's plate can be served from the same pan if **no salt is added during cooking** and salt is added only at the table by the other partner.
- Salty condiments (soy sauce, pickles, cheese) should be plated separately, never mixed into the shared dish.
