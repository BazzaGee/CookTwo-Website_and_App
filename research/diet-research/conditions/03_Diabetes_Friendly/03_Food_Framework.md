# Diabetes-Friendly Diet — Food Framework

## The diabetes plate method

A simple, effective structure used by diabetes educators:

- **½ plate non-starchy vegetables** — leafy greens, broccoli, cauliflower, peppers, tomatoes, cucumber, courgette, green beans, mushrooms, cabbage.
- **¼ plate protein** — fish, poultry, eggs, legumes, tofu, plain Greek yogurt.
- **¼ plate carbohydrate-rich food** — legumes, brown rice, quinoa, barley, oats, whole-grain bread, sweet potato, corn, peas.
- **Water or unsweetened tea/coffee** — no sugary drinks.

## Per-meal carbohydrate guide

| Meal | Default carbohydrate range |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | 30–45 g |
| Lunch | 30–60 g |
| Dinner | 30–60 g |
| Snacks | 0–15 g |

This is a **moderate default**, not a prescription. People on insulin may count carbohydrates differently; the app must not set insulin doses.

## Choose freely

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Non-starchy vegetables | Leafy greens, broccoli, cauliflower, peppers, tomatoes, cucumber, courgette, asparagus, mushrooms, cabbage, green beans |
| Protein | Fish, poultry, eggs, tofu, tempeh, lentils, chickpeas, beans, plain Greek yogurt |
| Whole grains | Brown rice, quinoa, barley, oats, buckwheat, whole-grain bread, whole-wheat pasta |
| Fruit | Berries, apples, pears, oranges, kiwi, peaches — whole fruit, moderate portions |
| Fats | Olive oil, avocado, nuts, seeds, oily fish |
| Drinks | Water, sparkling water, unsweetened tea, black coffee |
| Flavour | Herbs, spices, citrus, vinegar, garlic, chilli |

## Limit or avoid

| Category | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sugary drinks | Soft drinks, sweetened juice, energy drinks, sweet tea, lemonade | Rapid glucose spikes |
| Added sugar | Sugar, honey, maple syrup, agave, corn syrup, molasses | High glycaemic impact |
| Sweets and desserts | Cake, cookies, pastries, ice cream, candy, chocolate bars | Added sugar + refined flour |
| Refined grains | White bread, white rice, white pasta, instant noodles, refined cereals | High GI, low fibre |
| Sweetened dairy | Flavoured yogurt, sweetened milk, condensed milk | Added sugar |
| Sugary condiments | Ketchup, BBQ sauce, sweet chilli sauce, honey mustard, teriyaki | Hidden sugar |
| Fruit juice | Orange, apple, grape juice | Concentrated sugar, low fibre |
| Processed snacks | Cereal bars, granola bars, sweetened granola, biscuits | Added sugar + refined starch |
| Alcohol | Beer, wine, spirits | Hypoglycaemia risk, especially without food |

## Smart swaps

| Instead of | Choose |
|---|---|
| White rice | Brown rice, quinoa, barley, cauliflower rice |
| White bread | Whole-grain or sourdough bread |
| Sugary cereal | Plain oats with nuts and berries |
| Fruit juice | Whole fruit |
| Flavoured yogurt | Plain yogurt with berries |
| Ketchup/BBQ sauce | Tomato, herbs, vinegar, mustard (check label) |
| Crisps | Unsalted nuts, vegetable sticks, hummus |
| Milk chocolate | A small square of dark chocolate (≥70%) after a meal |

## Meal patterns

- **Breakfast:** plain oats with berries and walnuts; eggs with spinach and whole-grain toast; plain Greek yogurt with seeds.
- **Lunch:** lentil salad with olive oil and lemon; chicken and quinoa bowl with roasted vegetables; whole-grain wrap with tuna and salad.
- **Dinner:** baked salmon with barley and broccoli; chicken stir-fry with brown rice and vegetables; bean chilli with quinoa.
- **Snacks:** nuts, plain yogurt, vegetable sticks, hummus, a small apple with peanut butter.

## Couple cooking

- Cook the shared base with a moderate carbohydrate portion (e.g. 1 cup cooked brown rice for two) and let the non-diabetic partner add extra carbohydrate on their plate.
- Keep sugary drinks and desserts out of the shared meal; they can be added separately if the other partner chooses, but never mixed into the dish.
