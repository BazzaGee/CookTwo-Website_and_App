# Gluten-Free Diet — Food Framework

## Core plate structure

A safe plate is built from naturally gluten-free whole foods:

- **½ plate vegetables and fruit** — all fresh, frozen (plain) and unprocessed produce.
- **¼ plate protein** — fresh meat, poultry, fish, seafood, eggs, legumes, plain tofu (check labels), tempeh (certified GF).
- **¼ plate gluten-free carbohydrate** — rice, corn, quinoa, buckwheat, millet, sorghum, teff, amaranth, potato, sweet potato, certified GF oats, GF pasta/bread.
- **Fat** — olive oil, avocado, nuts, seeds, butter (plain).
- **Flavour** — herbs, spices (single-ingredient), citrus, vinegar (except malt), tamari or GF soy sauce.

## Safe staples

| Category | Safe examples |
|---|---|
| Grains | Rice (all forms), corn/maize, quinoa, buckwheat, millet, sorghum, teff, amaranth, certified GF oats |
| Flours | Rice flour, cornmeal, chickpea flour, almond flour, coconut flour, buckwheat flour, certified GF oat flour |
| Starches | Potato, sweet potato, cassava, tapioca, arrowroot |
| Protein | Fresh/frozen meat, poultry, fish, seafood, eggs, plain legumes, plain tofu, certified GF tempeh |
| Dairy | Plain milk, plain yogurt, cheese (check additives), butter |
| Fats | Olive oil, avocado oil, coconut oil, butter, ghee, nuts, seeds |
| Condiments | Tamari (GF soy sauce), apple cider vinegar, wine vinegar, rice vinegar, balsamic vinegar, herbs, spices, citrus |
| Sweeteners | Sugar, honey, maple syrup (plain) |

## Must avoid

- Wheat in every form: flour, wholemeal, durum, semolina, spelt, kamut, farro, emmer, einkorn, bulgur, couscous, orzo.
- Barley, rye, triticale.
- Malt, malt extract, malt syrup, malt vinegar, brewer's yeast.
- Regular soy sauce, teriyaki, hoisin, oyster sauce (unless labelled GF).
- Bread, pasta, noodles, couscous, breadcrumbs, panko, batter, pastry, pizza, crackers, pretzels (unless GF).
- Beer, ale, lager (unless GF).
- Seitan and any "wheat protein" product.
- Sausages, meatballs, deli meats and processed products containing rusk or fillers (unless GF).
- Bouillon and stock cubes (unless GF).

## Cross-contact rules for cooking

1. Use a clean board, pan and utensils; never share a toaster or colander with gluten foods.
2. Do not use the same pasta water, fryer oil or flour dusting station.
3. Thicken with cornstarch, arrowroot, rice flour or chickpea flour — never wheat flour.
4. Check shared condiments (jam, mustard, butter) for breadcrumb contamination.
5. At restaurants, confirm GF preparation and dedicated fryers; avoid buffets and shared fryers.

## Label rules

- Prefer products labelled "gluten-free".
- "Wheat-free" does **not** mean gluten-free (barley/rye may be present).
- "May contain wheat" is not permitted on a product carrying a GF claim; advisory labels on non-GF products are a warning sign.
- Ingredient names that signal gluten: wheat, barley, rye, malt, triticale, spelt, kamut, durum, semolina, bulgur, couscous, seitan, modified wheat starch.
- Vinegar is generally safe except malt vinegar; distilled vinegar is GF.

## Meal patterns

- **Breakfast:** certified GF oats with fruit and nuts; eggs with vegetables; yogurt with fruit and seeds; smoothie with GF ingredients.
- **Lunch:** rice or quinoa bowls with protein and vegetables; GF wraps (corn tortillas); salads with oil/vinegar dressing.
- **Dinner:** fish or chicken with potatoes/rice and vegetables; lentil curry with rice; stir-fry with tamari; GF pasta with tomato-based sauce.
- **Snacks:** fruit, nuts, plain yogurt, rice cakes, vegetable sticks with hummus (check label).

## Couple cooking

- Build the shared base naturally GF (rice, potatoes, vegetables, protein) and add gluten-containing bread, pasta or soy sauce only on the non-GF partner's plate.
- Never "just pick around" gluten — crumbs and sauces contaminate the whole dish.
